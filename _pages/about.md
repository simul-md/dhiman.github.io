I am a theoretical and computational physicist working on the quantum dynamics of light nuclei and excited electrons in molecules, clusters and at surfaces. My work sits where quantum mechanics has to be kept for some degrees of freedom but cannot be afforded for all of them, and I build and test hybrid methods that make that compromise controlled rather than ad hoc.

Research
======

**Nuclear quantum effects and quantum thermal baths.** Quantum thermal baths give every vibrational mode its correct quantum energy at the cost of classical molecular dynamics, which makes them excellent for structure and spectra. They are much less reliable for rates and transport. I study why, and I am developing hybrid quantum–classical schemes that keep the quantum statistics of these baths while recovering correct dynamics. At Université Gustave Eiffel I applied the adaptive quantum thermal bath and path-integral molecular dynamics to the electronic spectra of acrolein in the gas phase and in water.

**Hydrogen at graphene.** Atomic hydrogen can physisorb above a graphene sheet or chemisorb onto a carbon atom, and the traffic between the two channels controls sticking and diffusion. At ISMO (Université Paris-Saclay) I built an interaction potential covering both channels and computed sticking probabilities with mixed quantum–classical dynamics.

**Excited rare-gas clusters.** During my PhD at CIMAP in Caen I developed a hole–particle pseudopotential model for excited neutral argon clusters, and used it to compute their geometries, absorption and luminescence spectra, and non-adiabatic relaxation dynamics. I continue this work on Rydberg excitations and on the dissociation channels of excited clusters.

**Methods I use:** mixed quantum–classical and non-adiabatic dynamics (surface hopping, Ehrenfest, hybrid trajectories), wavepacket propagation and MCTDH, quantum thermal baths and path-integral molecular dynamics, *ab initio* electronic structure (MOLCAS, Gaussian, Quantum ESPRESSO), and scientific programming in C and Python.

Background
======

I received my PhD in physics from Normandie Université in 2022, after a master's degree at Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University with an Erasmus+ semester at the Technical University of Munich. Since then I have held postdoctoral positions at Goethe University Frankfurt, at ISMO (CNRS / Université Paris-Saclay) and at MSME, Université Gustave Eiffel.
