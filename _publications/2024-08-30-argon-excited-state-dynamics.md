We introduce a model for excitation dynamics with quantum transitions in neutral rare-gas clusters, using a diabatised diatomics-in-molecules (Di-DIM) basis with spin–orbit coupling. Adiabatic potential energy curves are evaluated on the fly, the electronic coefficients are evolved in the basis of a time-averaged Hamiltonian to minimise perturbations, and surface hopping handles transitions between adiabatic surfaces.

Applied to Ar₃*, Ar₇* and Ar₁₃* after excitation in the lowest singlet state of the 4s band, the dynamics show relaxation to a lower triplet state in all three clusters. Emission of an excited atom is the dominant dissociation channel, followed by excimer (Ar₂*) formation and then atomisation; whether an excimer forms depends on the initial conditions and on cluster size. No stable excited trimer isomer is found with Di-DIM. The results are compared with earlier studies of ionic clusters of similar size and with experiments on argon clusters and solid argon.
