Doctoral thesis carried out at CIMAP (GANIL–CEA–CNRS, Caen) under the supervision of Benoît Gervais. It develops a model Hamiltonian for excited neutral argon clusters and uses it to study their geometries, absorption and luminescence spectra, and their relaxation through non-adiabatic dynamics.
