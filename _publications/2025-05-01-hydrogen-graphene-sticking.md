Hydrogen meets graphene through two very different channels: a shallow physisorption well above the sheet and a chemisorbed state that requires the carbon atom to pucker out of plane. We construct an interaction potential that describes both channels and the barrier between them, and use it in mixed quantum–classical dynamics to compute sticking probabilities as a function of collision energy, with the hydrogen coordinate treated quantum mechanically against a vibrating surface.
