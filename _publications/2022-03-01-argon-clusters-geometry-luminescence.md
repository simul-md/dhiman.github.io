Small argon clusters are a clean model for how an electronic excitation in a weakly bound solid relaxes and emits. Using a hole–particle pseudopotential model Hamiltonian, we determine the structures of small neutral argon clusters in their ground and excited states and compute their absorption and luminescence spectra, connecting the excited-state geometries to the observed emission.
