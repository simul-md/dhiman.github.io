Excited rare-gas clusters relax by localising the excitation on a small excimer-like core, and the accuracy of that picture depends on how well the model describes the Rydberg states involved. This letter revisits the hole–particle pseudopotential model for neutral argon clusters, corrects its description of Rydberg excitations against high-level *ab initio* reference data, and follows how the excitation localises from the trimer up to larger clusters.
